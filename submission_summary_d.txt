Here is the detailed summary of the Phase D implementation:

1. **Fixed Assets & Depreciation Engine**
   - Implemented `FixedAsset` and `DepreciationScheduleEntry` domain entities.
   - Built `FixedAssetDepreciationService` algorithms mathematically enforcing Salvage Value bounds across three modes:
     - **Straight-Line:** Evenly splits book value depletion dynamically across active lifespans.
     - **Double Declining Balance:** Uses accelerated fraction matching $(2/N) * BookValue$, gracefully sliding into Straight-line when mathematically optimal.
     - **Sum-of-the-Years'-Digits:** Decrements fractional depreciation mapping precisely $N - t + 1$.

2. **Multi-Currency Ledger & Forex Revaluation**
   - Engineered the foundational `Currency` and `ExchangeRate` objects managing multi-currency relationships tracking effective dates dynamically.
   - Constructed `ForexRevaluationService` evaluating foreign outstanding balances on `SalesOrder` Invoices. Outputs **Unrealized Gain/Loss** differences from origin invoice rates vs Period End effective rates.
   - Outputs **Realized Gain/Loss** by calculating settlement discrepancies on cash collection actions dynamically matching base currencies.

3. **Regional Tax & VAT Computation Engine**
   - Created `TaxRule.cs` allowing flat vs `IsCompound` hierarchical dependencies simulating complex QST/GST tax scenarios.
   - Designed `TaxComputationService` rendering detailed VAT Returns summing `Output VAT` arrays against inbound `Input VAT` ledgers to render accurate jurisdictional `NetVatLiability` payouts.

4. **Deep Financials UI Integration**
